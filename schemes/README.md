### Electrical Schemes

This folder contains the electrical schematics developed throughout the construction of our **WRO Future Engineers 2026 vehicle**.

Instead of documenting only the final circuit, we kept the previous versions to show how our wiring architecture evolved through **planning, testing, troubleshooting, and refinement**.

### From Idea to Final Design

Our electrical design started with a simple hand-drawn schematic and was progressively updated as components were tested and integrated into the physical robot.

The different versions represent the main stages of this process:

| Stage                   | Schematic                          | Purpose                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **01 — Initial Design** | `hand_drawn_initial_schematic.jpg` | First connection plan and identification of the main electrical components. |
| **02 — Wiring V1**      | `previous_wiring_schematic_v1.png` | First digital representation of the prototype wiring.                       |
| **03 — Wiring V2**      | `previous_wiring_schematic_v2.png` | Updated connections after physical testing and design changes.              |
| **04 — Current Design** | `current_wiring_schematic.png`     | Final wiring configuration used for the vehicle integration.                |

### Design Evolution

Keeping the different schematic versions allowed us to document our design decisions instead of only presenting the final result.

Through this process, we were able to:

* Organize the power and signal connections.
* Identify and correct wiring problems during testing.
* Improve component accessibility and maintenance.
* Refine the electrical architecture before final PCB integration.
* Keep a clear reference for future modifications and troubleshooting.

### Documentation Structure

The **main README** explains the complete power and sensor architecture, including component selection, power distribution, sensor placement, calibration, PCB integration, and testing.

This folder focuses specifically on the **electrical schematics and their evolution**, providing the detailed connection references used throughout the development of the vehicle.
